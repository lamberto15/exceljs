In-cell checkboxes: `setCheckbox(ws, range, checked?)` and `style.checkbox` (Excel 365 2024+; boolean cells with the checkbox format, written with the feature property bag part and read back). Pictures: `fitImage(ws, id, range)` scales a picture into a cell or range (aspect kept, centered), `imageSize(bytes)` reads PNG / JPEG / GIF sizes, anchors accept ExcelJS-style fractional positions (`{ col: 1.5, row: 2.25 }`) and `{ tl, br }` corners (pictures and charts).
