Add column auto-fit: `autoFitColumns(ws, opts?)` sizes columns from their displayed contents (number formats, dates, rich text, bold/size, wrap, merges), and `estimateColumnWidths(rows, opts?)` computes widths from a sample for the streaming writer. Available from both `turbosheet` and `turbosheet/lite`; not included in a bundle unless imported.
