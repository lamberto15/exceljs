`sortRange(ws, range, keys, { header })` sorts the rows of a range like Excel; `fillTemplate(wb, data)` fills `{{placeholders}}` and repeats `{{items[].field}}` rows (totals ranges grow over the copies); `addDataValidation` moves lists over Excel's 255-character limit (or with commas) to a hidden `_lists` sheet instead of throwing; ExcelJS migration helpers `actualRowCount`, `lastRow`, `getRows`, `getSheetValues`.
